Should Athletes Take Creatine?

Jack Anderson, MS, CSCS · November 11, 2024

Creatine is one of the most studied supplements on the planet and has numerous benefits for athletic performance including:

  • Improved strength and power

  • Faster recovery

  • Increased explosiveness

  • Possible cognitive benefits

With such a wide range of positive adaptations, all high school athletes should consider using creatine, provided they have no kidney-related illnesses or issues.

Ensure that any supplements taken are NSF certified and only purchase creatine monohydrate with no additional ingredients. Here is a list of some of the best creatine products on the market:

  • Thorne

  • Klean

  • Momentous (Jack uses this brand)

  • Core (Gummies instead of powder)

Athletes should take 5g of creatine monohydrate daily. Some brands encourage a loading phase, but this is unnecessary.

Remember that supplements are a useful tool, but not a substitute for a proper nutrition and sleep.
