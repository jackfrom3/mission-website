NCAA Eligibility Requirements

Jack Anderson, MS, CSCS · October 28, 2024

Jack recently did a podcast outlining the academic requirements for high school athletes planning to play in NCAA or NAIA sports in college. You can listen to it right here.

In addition, here are a few links that provide more information directly from the NCAA.

  • NCAA Eligibility Center

  • Find your high school’s core courses approved by the NCAA

  • DI Academic Requirements

  • DII Academic Requirements

  • More about amateurism status

Athletes, it is critical to speak with your school counsellor and ensure you understand the timeline of events for each year of high school. Do not fall behind and see that all obligations are met on time. The recruiting process is challenging enough and this will make it easier on yourself, college coaches and admissions departments.
